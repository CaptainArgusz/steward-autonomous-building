> All findings concern the fictional PLATO-40 reference building and its synthetic data. They

## How to write a finding

```
### <C|P>-<nn> · <short title>
**Finding.** What the data shows, with numbers.
**Evidence.** Files, columns, time ranges, and the comparison made (what it was compared against).
**Recommendation.** What CLARA should do differently / what PLATO should build differently.
**Expected benefit.** Qualitative, or "simulated estimate: …".
**Uncertainty.** What could explain this instead; what data would settle it. Confidence 0–1.
```
